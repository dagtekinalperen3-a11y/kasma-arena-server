## English

**App name (30):** EvolveArena: Arena Survivor

**Short description (Play, 80):**
Survive endless monster waves, evolve your build and take down epic bosses!

**Subtitle (App Store, 30):** Survive. Evolve. Break records.

**Full description:**
EvolveArena is a fast, one-thumb arena survival game! Drop into endless waves of monsters —
your weapons fire on their own, you dodge, collect and grow stronger.

⚔️ 20 WEAPONS, 29 TOMES — Axes, whips, tornadoes, lightning, meteors, ghost blades,
wolf packs… Pick one of three cards every level and craft your own devastating combo.

👑 6 BOSSES & THE HELL GATE — From wave 10 a giant boss arrives every 5 waves. Beat the
wave 25 boss and the Hell Gate opens: harder, redder, more rewarding.

🗺️ 4 MAPS — Stone Plaza, Frozen Valley, Emerald Jungle, Crimson Desert, each with its own
monsters, rules and achievements.

🦸 25 HEROES — Every hero has a unique ultimate and perks: a cosmic warrior who opens black
holes, a shadow assassin who stops time, a crimson falcon raining arrows…

💎 PERMANENT PROGRESS — Earn gems, unlock heroes, gain permanent mastery, complete 52
achievements and ban the weapons you don't want.

🏆 WORLD RANKING — Optionally create an account and compete with the world.

📱 BUILT FOR MOBILE — Smooth 60 FPS, big touch controls, left-handed mode, notch support,
haptics, fully offline. No ads.
